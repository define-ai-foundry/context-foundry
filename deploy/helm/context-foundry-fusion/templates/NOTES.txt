View logs
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -f deploy/{{ include "context-foundry-fusion.fullname" . }}

Continuous replay feed (default)
  With sources.loop.enabled=true (default) the replay restarts every
  {{ .Values.sources.loop.delaySeconds }}s, so the Deployment runs as a healthy long-lived feed
  (no exit/restart CrashLoopBackOff). The engine rebuilds its tracker each pass.
  Point at live sensors instead with sources.sapient.enabled / sources.cot.enabled.

Wire Keycloak auth for the WebTAK-WS sink
  --set sinks.takWs.auth.keycloakTokenUrl=https://<iam>/realms/<realm>/protocol/openid-connect/token \
  --set sinks.takWs.auth.oidcClientId=<client-id> \
  --set sinks.takWs.auth.oidcClientSecret.secretName=<existing-secret> \
  --set sinks.takWs.auth.oidcClientSecret.secretKey=oidc-client-secret \
  --set sinks.takWs.verifyTls=true
  (Secrets are sourced via secretKeyRef and referenced in args as $(OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET);
   the literal never appears in the pod spec. For dev you may pass
   sinks.takWs.auth.oidcClientSecret.value=<secret> and the chart mints a Secret.)
